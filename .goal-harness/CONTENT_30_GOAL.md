# CONTENT 30 + 5-HOUR SCHEDULE GOAL

## Final Deliverable

Create thirty additional research-backed Korean middle-aged women's health articles, independently verify each at the established 99-point standard, integrate them into the Next.js site, and release them automatically at five-hour intervals after the currently public baseline.

## User Value

Readers receive a steady stream of distinct, source-backed health guides instead of a single bulk publication event. Search, topic pages, sitemap, direct routes, and structured data expose only articles whose release time has arrived.

## Required Features

- Exactly 30 accepted title and article-contract rows before prose drafting.
- Five-article dry run across multiple clusters before scaling.
- Per article: at least 3,500 visible Korean characters, 3–5 research runs, 5–8 sources, at least two official/primary YMYL sources, claim ledger, reader-visible citations, 3–5 valid internal links, and independent QA score 99.
- Zero blocking contract, title-template, source, UTF-8, link, or seven-token overlap defects.
- Five-hour Asia/Seoul release schedule recorded durably for all 30 articles.
- Future articles excluded from lists, search, topics, sitemap, metadata, and direct public routes until their release time; they become visible automatically without manual redeploy.
- Existing public articles remain available.

## Non-Goals

- No promise of Google ranking or traffic.
- No diagnosis, prescription, personalized medical treatment, invented clinical review, or AdSense console action.
- No DNS mutation.
- No thin keyword variants or shared prose template.

## Done Conditions

- Manifest reports 30 done, failed 0, review_needed 0, score 99 for every row.
- Strict audits at windows 5, 20, and full batch pass with zero warnings.
- Typecheck and production build pass.
- Time-boundary tests prove pre-release 404/hidden behavior and post-release discovery.
- Vercel production deployment is READY and representative current/future boundary routes are live-verified.
- Handoff records schedule, rollback deployment, validation, and deferred DNS state.

## User-Visible Result

The site keeps its current public library and automatically adds one new high-quality guide every five hours until all thirty scheduled articles have been released.
